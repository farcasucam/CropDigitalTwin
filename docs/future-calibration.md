# Future Calibration Plan

Before operational use, collect per plot and season:

- timestamped weather and greenhouse sensor data;
- soil VWC, rooting depth, field capacity, wilting point, drainage and
  nutrient measurements;
- irrigation/fertilization logs and actuator response/capacity;
- cover, geometry, thermal mass, ventilation and shading inventory;
- cultivar/rootstock identity and phenology observations;
- LAI, biomass partition, yield and harvest-quality observations;
- frost/heat exposure and observed recovery or damage.

Fit one parameter family at a time: environment/soil, phenology, radiation
and LAI, water, nutrients, then stress/damage. Keep calibration and validation
seasons separate, preserve source IDs, and only promote a parameter from
engineering approximation to calibrated after replay and residual checks.
