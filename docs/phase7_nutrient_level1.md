# Phase 7 / nutrients, level 1: an optional nitrogen model (placeholder parameters) and fertigation (PT-8a)

Status: DEVELOPMENT. **Every parameter of the model is an unsourced placeholder** (`NitrogenParameters.evidence_status = UNSOURCED_PLACEHOLDER`, requires calibration): nothing was read from a publication or fitted to data, and none is claimed for any crop. The results are properties of the STRUCTURE (sign, order, compensation, interaction), never agronomic values. Code `domain/nitrogen_demand.py`, option `model_options.nutrient_model` (`application/model_options.py`), tests `tests/test_nitrogen_demand.py`, script `manual_phase7_pt8a_fertigation.py`, report `data/xai/effects_pt8a_fertigation.json`.

## Scope decision behind it
A model per crop and situation from the literature is not feasible (parameters verifiable for tomato only, none found in the review for pepper, lettuce nitrate or blossom-end rot; no open nitrogen-trial data to calibrate). So: one general, simplified nitrogen model whose structure is defensible and whose numbers are declared placeholders, to give the explanation layer a fertilisation player with a credible mechanism. The review's reading of full texts is not needed for that; it would be needed only to cite a coefficient.

## The model (opt-in; the default runs are unchanged)
Request option `model_options: {"nutrient_model": "NITROGEN_DEMAND", "soil_nitrogen_initial_kg_ha": <optional>}` (default `REDUCED_POOL`, no block, hashes unchanged).
* State: the soil mineral nitrogen pool (`nutrient_available_kg_ha`, no longer capped at a reserve; the state's invariant is kept by letting the reserve follow the largest pool) and the nitrogen held by the crop (`CropGrowthState.plant_nitrogen_kg_ha`, absent from `to_dict` when None, so historical states keep their representation).
* Per step: the dose is added to the soil pool; the crop wants the CRITICAL nitrogen of the biomass it is growing towards (`10 * a * W^(1-b)` kg/ha, W in t/ha of dry biomass; the form of a critical dilution curve) and takes `min(demand, soil pool)`; the soil pool then loses a fraction equal to `leaching per mm * the drainage of the step` (the water balance's drainage, computed in the same step); the nitrogen nutrition index NNI = crop nitrogen / critical nitrogen (capped at 1) is the growth factor and the status.
* Series: the five of level 0 (the pool is now the soil nitrogen) plus `plant_nitrogen_kg_ha` and `critical_nitrogen_kg_ha`, available only with this model (otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE, so the default catalogue and trajectories do not move). Roles: past-only covariates.
* Simplifications that matter: one pool, no mineralisation, no volatilisation, no root-depth dependence, the index is evaluated against the potential growth of the step, the crop starts at its critical nitrogen (index 1).

## PT-8a: a dose, a heat wave and an irrigation together (truth only)
Tomato, outdoor, `NITROGEN_DEMAND` with the default initial soil pool (largely depleted by the window: the crop is nitrogen limited), 336 h from hour 1500, DEV seasons 34, 35, 37, 38. Players: heat wave (+6 degC), a dose of 40 kg/ha in total over 24 h, and an extra irrigation (0.6 mm per step, replacing the 0.38 plan). Exact Shapley over 8 coalitions plus the pairwise dose-irrigation interaction. The data-based comparison is not part of this package.
* **Biomass.** The net effect is only +6 to +10 g/m2 and hides a compensation: the dose gives +68 to +71, the heat wave costs -61 to -62, the extra irrigation +0.08 to +0.15 (nothing). The interaction among the players is 20 percent of the sum of absolute shares.
* **Nitrogen status (mean NNI).** +0.31 in total, almost all the dose (0.30), the heat wave 0.016 (a smaller crop leaves more nitrogen per unit of biomass).
* **Leached nitrogen.** 2.6 to 2.8 kg/ha in total (the dose 1.6 to 1.8, the irrigation 0.63 to 0.69, the heat wave 0.36 to 0.39). The interaction is 89 to 92 percent of the sum of absolute shares: leaching is the product of the pool and the drainage, so the pairwise dose-irrigation interaction is +34 to +35 percent. The extra irrigation raises the leaching in 4 of 4 seasons and the dose-irrigation interaction on the biomass is negative in 4 of 4, but it is tiny (about -0.6 g/m2).
* **Reading.** Fertigation has a visible STRUCTURE (the irrigation leaches part of the dose: a product, not a sum), but with these placeholders it is a small effect (about 5 percent of the dose leached, under 1 g/m2 of biomass). The size says nothing about real soils; the claim that remains is that the explanation layer can state "the irrigation leached part of the dose" with the interaction named and the size left as uncalibrated.

## What it does not do
No N/P/K, no fertiliser type or efficiency other than 1, no quality, no calibration, no other crops tested, no comparison with a data-based method in this package, the initial soil pool is the state's default (a request option exists to set it).
